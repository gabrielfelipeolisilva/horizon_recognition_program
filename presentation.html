Why

Purpose of the Program

🎯

Recognize impact

Reward contributions that create measurable value for the business, the culture and our customers.

⚖️

Same criteria for everyone, independent board evaluation and declared conflicts of interest.

🤝

Open to all

Any employee can nominate a colleague, a whole team or themselves — managers, peers or direct reports.

What

Three award categories

How

Scoring criteria

Relevance (up to 50 pts)

  • 15 Relevant — impact on one team or project.
  • 30 Significant — impact across multiple areas.
  • 50 Exceptional — organization-wide impact.

5 criteria (up to 50 pts)

  • S Exceptional — 10 pts
  • A Significant — 7 pts
  • B Relevant — 4 pts
Relevance + Σ 5 criteria = max. 100 pts Minimum of 60 points to be eligible

When

The process cycle

  1. 1

    Nominate

    Anyone submits a nomination with a live score (min. 60).

  2. 2

    Review

    People & Culture checks eligibility and moves it to review.

  3. 3

    Board

    Each board member scores independently and declares conflicts.

  4. 4

    Ranking

    Final score = average of the evaluations without conflict.

  5. 5

    Award

    Approved winners receive their prize by tier and position.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I nominate myself?

Yes. Choose "Myself" under "Who is this nomination for?" and your details are copied to the nominee section. Self-nominations are individual and go through the same board evaluation.

What happens if my nomination scores below 60?

It is not eligible: the platform explains the minimum, clears the form and nothing is saved.

Does the nominator's score count in the final result?

No. It is a reference only. The final score is the average of the board evaluations without conflict.

How is a group prize paid?

The tier amount is divided equally between all group members.

Where is my data stored?

In this prototype everything stays in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.
